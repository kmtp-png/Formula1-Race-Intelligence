04 / HOW TO READ IT

DATA FIRST.
HYPE SECOND.

This is an educational data-visualization project built around Formula 1 results and performance indicators. Charts are intended to help explore comparisons and historical patterns, not to claim a single metric explains every race outcome.

01EXPLOREChoose a chart, driver, team or season.
02COMPARELook for differences and patterns across groups.
03INTERPRETRead the metric and timeframe before drawing conclusions.
